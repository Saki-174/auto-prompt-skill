~~~powershell
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tests\windows_acceptance.ps1 -RuntimeArchive 'C:\downloads\python-3.13.16-embed-amd64.zip' -LegacyBundle 'C:\downloads\auto-prompt-skill-1.0.0-bundle.zip' -OutputDirectory 'C:\temporary\auto-prompt-evidence'
~~~
